How to play

Words need at least 3 letters. Regular plurals are accepted.

Each rescue starts with seven letters. Select letters to build a word. Letters refresh after every valid word you build.

Each letter adds one piece of wreckage. Build enough to save Gridda. Longer words score more points.

The first reshuffle in each round is free; subsequent reshuffles cost 5 Club Credits.

Club Credits can be earned in Rescue Challenge levels.

RISING TIDE

How to play

  • Tap letters to make a word of at least 3 letters, then build with it.
  • Every letter adds one block or plank. A 4-letter word scores 16 points; a 5-letter word scores 30, a 6-letter word 50, and a 7-letter word 75.
  • Used letters refill after each valid word. In Sinking Letters, lost slots never refill, even when reshuffling. You cannot repeat a word in the same run.
  • The first reshuffle is free. Further reshuffles cost 5 Club Credits. The hazard keeps moving.
